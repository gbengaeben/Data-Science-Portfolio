# ☕ Thermal Dynamics & Coffee Cooling Analysis

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) project examining how different cup configurations (insulated walls vs. lids vs. ceramic) impact thermal retention over time using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

## 📊 Project Overview
When enjoying a hot cup of coffee, how much does container design actually matter? This project analyzes temperature decline data over a 50-minute observation window to evaluate real-world thermodynamic behavior, evaporation effects, and cooling rates.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Libraries
* **Python** 
* **Pandas** (Data loading, indexing, and rate-of-change calculations via `.diff()`)
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (Professional data visualization styling, loop-based plotting, heatmaps, scatter regression, and color-gradient matrices)

## 📈 Visualizations & Key Findings

1. **Continuous Cooling Curves (Line Plot):**
   * *Finding:* The insulated cup *with a lid* drastically outperforms open containers in the first 10 minutes, proving that minimizing steam exposure and evaporation is critical for heat retention.
2. **Statistical Regression (Scatter Plot & Trendlines):**
   * *Finding:* Models individual data points against linear trends, demonstrating Newton's law of cooling in action.
3. **Thermal Gradient Matrix (Heatmap):**
   * *Finding:* Visualizes temperature evolution across all cup types simultaneously, highlighting the "Orange Zone" (optimal 55°C–65°C drinking range) and real-world data noise like staggered pouring times.
4. **Total Temperature Drop (Bar Chart):**
   * *Finding:* Summarizes total heat lost from minute 5 to minute 55 for quick executive-level comparison.
